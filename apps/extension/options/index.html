Connect your companion

The desktop app handles your AI provider, clipboard, shortcut and shared settings.

  1. Start AI Quick Answer on Windows.
  2. Open desktop Settings → Extension pairing & diagnostics.
  3. Enter the displayed pairing token below.

Not connected

API keys and Gemini cookies stay encrypted in the desktop app. The extension stores only its pairing token in trusted extension storage. Copied text is sent to your selected AI provider when you trigger the assistant. Normal paste is preserved by the desktop listener. The browser-only fallback is Ctrl+Shift+Y; change it in chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
